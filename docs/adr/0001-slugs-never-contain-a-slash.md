# Slugs never contain a slash

A Slug is derived from a Post's or Bookmark's title, and until October 2026 the rule kept `/`, so "On/Off" was published at `/blog/on/off/` while its Preview image and content file used `on-off`. We decided that a Slug never contains `/` (or `\`): those characters become `-` everywhere, including heading anchors. This revises the 2026-10 exec plan's "keep title-derived permalinks" decision only for titles containing a slash; URLs stay title-derived.

## Consequences

- The seven Posts with a slash in their title moved (for example to `/blog/on-off/`). Their old URLs were added to their `redirectFrom` front matter rather than generated by a leftover rule, so the old rule could be deleted. Their canonical URLs changed and Sequoia updated their seven records.
- Heading anchors containing a slash changed (for example `#blank-screen-during-after-boot`). Fragments cannot be redirected; the chance of an off-site link to one was judged negligible.
